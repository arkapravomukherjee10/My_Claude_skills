# Official sources by regulator

Only these domains count as "verified".

**Known fetch problems:** irdai.gov.in, npci.org.in (current circular pages and PDFs), meity.gov.in, egazette.gov.in, SEBI listing pages and RBI PDF links often block automated fetching or show CAPTCHAs. RBI and SEBI HTML listing pages sometimes serve stale content. Use the verification ladder in SKILL.md (site: search for an alternative official URL, then the browser) before marking anything unverified. Without a browser, current NPCI circulars usually can't be confirmed — NPCI's public listing pages lag by a year or more.

Section names on regulator sites change from time to time — if a listing page moves, search `site:<domain> <keyword> <month year>` to find it again. Confirm everything on the domain listed here.

## Core tier (always sweep)

| Regulator | Official domain(s) | Where new items appear | Typical relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RBI** — Reserve Bank of India | rbi.org.in | Notifications (circulars), Master Directions, Press Releases, Draft Directions / "Drafts for comments", FAQs | Lending, NBFC/MFI, digital lending, KYC, payments, PA/PG, fair practices, recovery, outsourcing, IT/cyber |
| **SEBI** — Securities and Exchange Board of India | sebi.gov.in | Legal → Circulars, Master Circulars, Regulations; Reports → Consultation Papers; Enforcement orders | Securities, MFs, brokers, investment advisers, KYC (KRA), NBFC debt listings, AIF |
| **IRDAI** — Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India | irdai.gov.in, policyholder.gov.in (IRDAI's consumer site) | Circulars, Regulations, Master Circulars, Exposure Drafts | Insurance distribution, bancassurance, corporate agents, credit-life / group insurance attached to loans, claims, digital sales |
| **NPCI** — National Payments Corporation of India | npci.org.in | Circulars by product (UPI, NACH, AePS, IMPS, RuPay, NETC, UPI AutoPay) | UPI flows, mandates, AutoPay/e-NACH for EMI collection, AePS for rural collections |
| **NBBL** — NPCI Bharat BillPay Ltd (Bharat Connect, formerly BBPS) | bharat-connect.com (current), bharatbillpay.com (older pages), npci.org.in | Circulars / guidelines | BBPS loan-repayment billers, biller onboarding, complaint TAT |

## Extended tier (sweep every run; often quiet)

| Regulator | Official domain(s) | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **PFRDA** | pfrda.org.in | NPS/APY circulars — relevant if distributing pension products |
| **MeitY / Data Protection Board** | meity.gov.in, dpdpa / DPB pages on meity.gov.in | DPDP Act/Rules notifications, consent manager rules, phased compliance dates — affects every consent and data flow |
| **FIU-IND** | fiuindia.gov.in | PMLA reporting (STR/CTR), AML/CFT guidelines, registration of reporting entities |
| **CERT-In** | cert-in.org.in | Cyber security directions, incident reporting timelines, advisories for financial sector |
| **MCA** | mca.gov.in | Companies Act amendments, NBFC-relevant corporate filings |
| **IFSCA** | ifsca.gov.in | GIFT City fintech / payments rules — usually Low relevance |
| **NHB** | nhb.org.in | Housing finance (HFC) items — mostly moved to RBI, occasional NHB circulars |
| **Ministry of Finance** (DFS, DEA, Revenue) | financialservices.gov.in, dea.gov.in, finmin.gov.in | Government schemes (PMJDY, PM SVANidhi, credit guarantee schemes), notifications affecting banks/NBFCs |
| **Official Gazette** | egazette.gov.in | Final notified Acts/Rules (e.g. DPDP Rules, PMLA rules) — use to confirm legal effect and date |
| **PIB** | pib.gov.in | Official government press releases — acceptable as an official source for announcements, but prefer the regulator's own document when one exists |

## Secondary tier — Self-Regulatory Organisations (label as SRO, not regulator)

| SRO | Domain | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **MFIN** (Microfinance Industry Network) — RBI-recognised SRO for NBFC-MFIs | mfinindia.org | Guardrails on lender count per borrower, indebtedness caps, collection conduct |
| **Sa-Dhan** — RBI-recognised SRO for microfinance | sa-dhan.net | Code of conduct updates for MFIs |
| **FACE** (Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment) — RBI-recognised SRO-FT | faceofindia.org | Digital lending code of conduct for fintechs/LSPs |

SRO codes aren't law, but RBI expects members to follow them and they often front-run RBI rules. Mark these as "SRO guidance" in the digest.

## Not verified sources (discovery only)

News sites (ET BFSI, Moneycontrol, Business Standard, Mint, Medianama, Inc42, Entrackr), law-firm alerts, LinkedIn posts, Twitter/X. Use them to find items; confirm on an official domain above before including.
